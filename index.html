Craft

We choose pieces from Vietnamese makers who still cut, bead and embroider by hand — silk, brocade and linen with a real hand-feel.

02

Fit

Every áo dài is measured in centimetres — bust, waist, hip, height — because Vietnamese cuts are not Australian S, M, L.

03

Occasion

Tết, the tea ceremony, a school photo, a wedding. We select for the moment, and tell you what each piece is for.

04

Care

Clear care notes, a reply within two business days, and advice on fit before you buy — by email, from Melbourne.
