Vietnamese áo dài are cut close through the bust and waist. If you sit between two sizes, choose the larger and we can advise on a tailor’s take-in.

How to measure

Bust.
Around the fullest part of the bust, tape level under the arms. Wear the bra you plan to wear with the áo dài.
Waist.
Around the narrowest part of the natural waist, usually just above the navel. Keep one finger under the tape.
Hip.
Around the fullest part of the hips, feet together.
Height.
Barefoot, back against a wall, from floor to the top of the head.
